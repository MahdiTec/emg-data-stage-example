# Protocol (example)

Written so that a person outside the group can repeat the recording.

1. **Sensor and site.** Gel electrodes of silver chloride, 2 cm apart, along the forearm flexors of the right arm. The ground electrode on the elbow bone. The skin is cleaned with alcohol first. Sensor model and gain: write them here. Source for the position: write here the page or paper your group used.
2. **Posture.** Seated. Forearm on the table, palm up.
3. **Task.** Close the hand into a fist and hold it during the action window. Relax during rest.
4. **Trial.** Rest 3 s, warning cue 1 s, action 4 s, rest 2 s. Total 10 s.
5. **Recording.** 5 trials without a pause. Total 50 s. One minute of pause between recordings.
6. **Cue.** The person watches `cue/cue-video.mp4`. The same video for every recording.
7. **Session.** New electrodes at the start, placed again from zero. 4 recordings. Six sessions on three days.
8. **Settings.** Sampling at 1000 Hz, measured from the time column. The file has two columns: `time_ms` and `emg_adc`.
9. **Filters.** A band-pass filter: write its type and its two limits here, with the source. The upper limit must be below half of the sampling rate. A notch filter at 60 Hz. If your sensor gives a smoothed signal (an envelope), say so here. Then 50 to 100 samples per second are enough, and no band-pass filter is needed.
10. **Repeat a recording when** the person moves at the wrong time, an electrode comes loose, or the file has fewer samples than planned.
11. **Consent.** Each participant agreed to the recording. We use codes, never names.
